# Party Members (AI Companions)
 
*The DM voices these two consistently every session. Feel free to rename/redesign before session 1.*
 
## 1. Bex — the Overconfident Rogue-Mechanic
- **Vibe:** Fast-talking, self-taught tinkerer, thinks every problem can be solved by hitting it or hacking it.
- **Personality:** Sarcastic, loyal underneath it, secretly worried she's not as good as she brags.
- **Quirk:** Names all her gadgets. Currently arguing with one that keeps beeping at her.
- **Relationship to player:** Met recently, still earning trust, but already has your back.
## 2. Thrum — the Deadpan Warrior-Monk
- **Vibe:** Enormous, calm, unbothered by chaos. Speaks rarely, but every line lands.
- **Personality:** Dry humor, philosophical, weirdly good at cooking for someone built like a tank.
- **Quirk:** Collects small weird objects ("for luck") from every place the party visits.
- **Relationship to player:** Respects competence, warms up slowly, fiercely protective once earned.
## Party Dynamic
Bex and Thrum bicker like siblings but always show up when it matters. They should react honestly to player choices — approve, disagree, tease, worry — not just cheerlead.
