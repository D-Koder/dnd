## Port Halloway lower deck

### Fire Slime
- **Tier:** Weak
- **HP:** 8
- **AC:** 10
- **Attack Bonus:** +2
- **Damage:** d4 fire
- **Description:** A viscous, faintly glowing ooze that radiates heat — not hot enough to be dangerous on contact, but its heat-pulse attack scorches at range.
- **Loot Notes:** Occasionally leaves behind a residual ember core or scorched slime residue.
- **First Encountered:** Port Halloway lower deck
