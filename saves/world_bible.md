# World Bible

*This file is the persistent memory of the world. Update it as new places/factions/NPCs matter.*

## The Setting
**Name:** Veyrune
A fantasy world where old magic and crashed spaceship tech got mashed together a few centuries ago. Wizards debug spell-scrolls like code. Some swords have USB-C charging ports. Nobody fully understands the old tech, so it's treated like magic anyway.

## Currency
The standard currency is the **coil** — small stamped discs of copper, silver, and gold, re-minted from salvaged old-world wiring and valued partly for their conductivity as much as their worth. A hot meal runs 1-2 copper coils; a night's lodging around 1 silver coil; passage on a coastal trade ship 5-10 silver coils. Old-tech relics are often bartered directly rather than sold for coin, since their value is hard to standardize.

## Settlements
- **Port Halloway** (town) — A scrappy trade hub built around the crashed hull of a sky-ship. Bar, market, guild board. Confirmed to be one piece of a deliberately scattered fleet — partial designation "...N-3."
- **Bellcaster's Reach** (village) — A fishing and salvage-diving village on the same coastline as Port Halloway. Known for divers who work the shallow wrecks offshore.
- **Ironspire** (kingdom) — Capital of the Ashgrave Kingdom, the nearest major political power. A walled inland city, partly built from scavenged old-tech scaffolding.
- **The Rustlands** (outpost) — A stretch of open water further out past Bellcaster's Reach, littered with wreckage from the scattered fleet. Dangerous, sparsely charted — rumored to hold more Anchor sites.

## Known Locations
- Port Halloway's dock office — Old Corr's post, heart of the town's daily business.
- The sealed lower deck — beneath Port Halloway, inside the crashed hull. Still-powered console inside confirms the "scuttle pattern" (the fleet scattered deliberately so not everything died at once).
- A second, smaller sealed door beyond the console room — no visible socket, a residual energy trail newer than the surrounding dust. Marked (scan + chalk symbol) but not yet explored.

## Map Notes
*Relational/directional notes only, for consistency — not a visual map. Grows as the world is explored.*
- Port Halloway sits on Veyrune's southern coast, built directly into the crashed hull.
- Bellcaster's Reach is two days' sail north of Port Halloway, along the same coastline.
- Ironspire, capital of the Ashgrave Kingdom, is roughly four days inland (east) from Port Halloway via the old trade road.
- The Rustlands lie further out to sea past Bellcaster's Reach — no safe charted route, reachable only by experienced crews willing to risk the wreckage field.

## Notable Artifacts
- Raken's relic fragment — reactive; warmed for the first time on contact with the ship's distress signal.
- The rusted key — has an embedded shard matching the relic fragment's energy signature; fits both the sealed hatch and a pedestal socket downstairs.
- The dormant egg-shaped object — found paired specifically to Raken's fragment via the pedestal socket; not yet activated.
- The glyph — three interlocking rings split by a jagged line. Appears on the key, the console, and Old Corr's hidden family disc.
- Old Corr's family disc — bears the same glyph; passed down through his family with a warning never to let anyone dig deep into the old hull.
- A smoking/etched bounty notice — old-tech, not normal paper. Found on the market board.
